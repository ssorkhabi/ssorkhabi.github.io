I am a research assistant in the [Insect Evolution and Genomics Group](https://www.heliconius.zoo.cam.ac.uk/){:target="_blank"} at the Department of Zoology, University of Cambridge. I completed my master's (by research) in the same lab, studying the repeatability of adaptation to altitude in two clades of tropical butterflies: Heliconiini and Ithomiini. During my undergrad days, I worked on the role of sexual antagonism in wing shape evolution in *Drosophila melanogaster*.

My research interests include population genomics, evolutionary ecology, adaptive potential, and climate change.
