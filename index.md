I am a research assistant in the [Insect Evolution and Genomics Group](https://www.heliconius.zoo.cam.ac.uk/){:target="_blank"} at the Department of Zoology, University of Cambridge. I completed my master's (by research) in the same lab, studying the repeatability of adaptation to altitude in two clades of tropical butterflies: Heliconiini and Ithomiini. As an undergrad, I worked on the role of sexual antagonism in wing shape evolution in *Drosophila melanogaster* in the [Reuter Lab](https://reuterlab.github.io/). More broadly, my research interests include population genomics, evolutionary ecology, adaptive potential, and climate change.
{: .lighter}

Inspired by the mentors whose help has shaped my career, I am passionate about accessibility, outreach, and widening participation. Outside of work, I read, craft, and wander around looking for cool plants and insects — sometimes drawing or photographing what I see (both quite amateurly).
{: .lighter}
